Codex Xenologica – Necrons · v1.4.2 · August 2026

Necron Codex Notes

Gaps, contradictions and typos found while entering the codex into the 30k list builder, with the call the builder makes on each one so the two can be brought back in line. Page numbers are the PDF's.

  • 6 items named but never printed
  • 10 contradictions
  • 6 detachments without unlock text
  • 4 unclear entries
  • 15 typos

01 Named but never printed

Each of these appears on a datasheet or in an option, but there's no rule text or weapon profile for it anywhere in the codex. The builder shows the name and flags it as undefined.

  1. 114–129

    Dynastic Protocols (X) special rule

    Given to most Dramatis Personae, with Nihilakh, Sautekh and other variants. p.115 and p.121 note that characters can join units with a different version, but the rule itself is never written out.

    Rule text for each dynasty value.

  2. 52, 77

    Interception Protocols special rule

    Listed on the Sentry Pylon and the Gauss Pylon.

    Rule text.

  3. 38, 62

    Phase-Tunnel Cutters wargear

    Listed on the Canoptek Tomb Stalker and Canoptek Tomb Sentinel. No profile, and no wargear text.

    Weapon profile, or wargear text if it isn't a weapon.

  4. 28

    Synaptic Disruptor weapon

    A Hierotek Circle option (+5 points each). There's a Synaptic Disintegrator profile on p.108 but no Disruptor.

    A profile, or confirmation that it should read Synaptic Disintegrator.

  5. 45

    Transdimensional Beamer weapon

    Canoptek Tomb Crawlers can swap their Twin Gauss Reaper for one for free. No profile.

    Weapon profile.

  6. 78, 100

    Transcendant C'Tan Shard's Power of the C'Tan

    The five named Shards each get a once-per-battle unique Power. The generic Shard's page has none, so the builder assumes it only rolls on the p.100 table.

    Confirmation that it's meant to have no unique Power.

02 Contradictions

Places where two parts of the codex disagree. The builder had to pick one, so each entry says which.

  1. 16

    Destroyer Lord in Skorpekh Frame: which claw?

    The Wargear list gives a Flensing Claw, but both claw options swap out a Photonic Assault Claw.

    Photonic Assault Claw, as the options describe.

  2. 28

    Hierotek Circle option names a model the unit doesn't have

    One option reads Any Immortal Guardian or Despotek. The unit has Hierotek Guardians, not Immortal Guardians, and the next option on the same page gets it right.

    Hierotek Guardian or Despotek.

  3. 41

    Immortals' weapon is called three different things

    The Wargear list gives a Gauss Blaster. The option swaps Gauss Flayers for Tesla Carbines.

    Gauss Blaster as standard, with the Tesla Carbine swap.

  4. 43, 106

    Canoptek Reanimators' beam

    The datasheet says Atomisation Beam. The profile on p.106 is Nanoscarab Atomisation Beam.

    Treated as the same weapon.

  5. 48

    Annihilation Barge: single or twin?

    The Wargear list gives a Tesla Destructor. The option swaps a Twin Tesla Destructor.

    Both spellings kept as printed, and treated as the same weapon.

  6. 29

    Cryptothralls' Expendable (1)

    It's printed under Unit Type rather than Special Rules.

    A special rule.

  7. 23

    Plasmancy page opens by naming Geomancy

    The intro reads accessible to models with the Geomancy Trait. The rest of the page says Plasmancy.

    Plasmancy.

  8. 126

    Imotekh's The Storm Cometh is printed in a Melee table

    Its columns are R/FP/RS/AP/D, and the rule calls it a Shooting Attack.

    A ranged weapon.

  9. 127

    Xun'Bakyr's statline is labelled Imotekh

    This looks like it was carried over from the previous page. The numbers themselves are hers.

    Her name on the profile.

  10. 129

    Noctilith Beacon or Beacons

    Szarekh's rule box says Noctilith Beacon. His Special Rules and Traits say Noctilith Beacons.

    Treated as one rule under both names.

03 Detachments without unlock text

The other Auxiliary Detachments say what unlocks them. These go straight from the title to their slots.

  1. 91

    Ghost Strider Phalanx

    Its only restriction is on its own Fast Attack slots (Tomb Blades only).

  2. 92

    Lokhust Phalanx, Skorpekh Phalanx, Ophydian Phalanx

    Each limits its own slots to Destroyer units. It isn't clear whether the Command slot that unlocks them must also hold a Destroyer, the way p.91 says an Auxiliary Detachment can require.

  3. 92

    Tomb Vigils, Reclamation Legion

    Only their own War Engine and Elites slots are restricted.

  4. 91–92

    For all six, any filled Command slot, like a core Auxiliary Detachment.

    The unlock condition for each.

04 Unclear entries

  1. 18

    Cryptek frame options have no names

    The three frame sub-options on p.18 aren't named.

    Descriptive labels: Striding (M8), Hovering (Cavalry, M12) and Phasing (M10).

  2. 118

    Valgûl's Eternal Warrior has no value

    Everywhere else it's printed as Eternal Warrior (X).

    The intended value.

  3. 76

    Seraptek Heavy Construct's profile has no T or W columns

    Its profile uses Front and Rear armour with I, A and HP, like a vehicle.

    Confirmation that it's meant to be an armoured profile.

  4. 110

    Cryptotendrils use numbers for AM and SM

    They're printed as 3 and 3. Other melee weapons write A and S or a modifier.

    Whether these should be fixed values or relative to the model's characteristics.
