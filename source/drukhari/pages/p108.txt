                                                                                                 Codex Xenologica – Drukhari (Horus Heresy 3rd edition)

                  FAQ

      Pain Engine Special Rule
       Q: If a Unit that includes models with the Pain
       Engine Special Rule has a Tactical Status, does it
        suffer the generic penalties for having a Tactical
        Status ?

       A: No. The Unit is unaffected by all effects
       of Tactical Status, including both the generic
        penalties for having a Tactical Status and the
        effects of the specific Tactical Status. The reason
       the Unit can gain Tactical Status at all is only to
       support itself and other Units with the Power
      from Pain special rule.
