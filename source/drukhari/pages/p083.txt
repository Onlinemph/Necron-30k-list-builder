       Drukhari Ranged Weapon Profiles

Las Weapons
Having experimented with the arcane energies harvested from their captive stars, the drukhari
supplemented their arsenal of las weaponry with darklight weapons; rather than the elegant beam of
the bright lance, these would fire a barely-controlled eldritch burst capable of permanently scarring the
retinas of those who beheld it unshielded.
