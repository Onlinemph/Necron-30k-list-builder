      Bladed Legs
           If a model with this Special Rule is Engaged by any enemy Models that are part of a Combat, it
          inflicts D6+1 Hits on all Units (friendly and enemy) in that Combat that do not include any Models
        with the Titan, Knight or Antigrav Sub-Type, rather than D3+1 (see the rules for Titans in the Assault
        Phase, Liber Questoris page 75)


     Tormentor Volley
     A model with this Special Rule that remains stationary in the controlling player's Movement Phase
      may fire two weapons with the Power Hungry Special Rule in the following Shooting Phase, rather
        than one. However, if doing so, the model may not fire other weapons in the same Shooting Phase.
