 Special Rules
 Precognisant
 During the first round of a Challenge involving Malys, the opposing player must always select the
  first Gambit, even if she is not the Challenger, and she gains Challenge Advantage. So long as Malys
  is involved in a Challenge, she always has Challenge Advantage and cannot lose it, and her opponent
 cannot gain Challenge Advantage.

 Mind Like a Steel Trap
 Whenever Malys' Unit is selected as the Target Unit of a Reaction made by the Opposing Player,
  Malys' Controlling Player may have the Reactive Unit, or one other Enemy Unit within 12" of that
  Unit, gain the Stunned status.
