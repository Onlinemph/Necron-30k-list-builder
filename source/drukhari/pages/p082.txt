    Torment (X)
     When making a Wound Test for a Weapon with
        this Special Rule against a Target Unit that is not
       entirely composed of Models with the Walker
     Type or Gargantuan Sub-Type, the result of the
     Wound Test before any modifiers are applied
      must be checked.
         If any Wound Test for a Weapon with the
      Torment (X) Special Rule results in a ‘5' or
         ‘6’ before any modifiers are applied, then all
      Models, except Walker and Gargantuan Models,
       in the Target Unit gain a Status at the end of
      Step 11 of the Shooting Attack process, Step 10
       of the Initiative Step process or Step 4 of the
      Challenge Process, regardless of wether any
     Damage was inflicted by the Attack. The Status
      gained is indicated by the value of X attached to
       that variant of the Torment (X) Special Rule.
