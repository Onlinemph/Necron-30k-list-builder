Splinter Weapons
Splinter weapons were a type of electro-catapult, of the same kind as asuryani shuriken weapon; rather
than monomolecular disks, however, the drukhari ammunition of choice was a clusted of crystallized
toxins, gradually shaved and projected at high speed as the gun fired.
