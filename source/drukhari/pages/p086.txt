     Haywire Weapons
      Haywire weapons fired electromagnetic bursts able to disable a vehicle’s control systems in but a few

    Heat Lances
86    The drukhari had uniquely discovered a way to meld lance and melta technologies. Such weapons had
       greater effective range than a regular melta weapon, and the same armour-piercing abilities as a lance,
      though their high energy consumption limited their potential.
