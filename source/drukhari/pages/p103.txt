 Special Rules
 Ten Moves Ahead
  If Vect is part of an army, then at the start of each of the Opposing Player's Turns, Vect's Controlling
  Player gains one additional Reaction Point so long as Vect has not been destroyed.

 Ancient Nemesis
  All Melee attacks made by Vect targeting a unit that includes any Models with the Asuryani,
  Anhrathe, Drukhari, Exodite or Saedath Trait gain +1 to the To Hit roll.

  Living Dark Muse
  If a Model with this Special Rule is part of an Army, then the Controlling Player may apply the
  following additional Special Rules to all Auxiliary, Apex and Primary Detachments that have the
  Drukhari Trait:
  •   If four or more Troops Battlefield Role Slots in the Primary Detachment of this Army are filled
    with units with the Kabals trait, then all Force Organisation Slots with the Troops Battlefield Role
     in the Primary Detachment are considered Prime Slots.
  •  Friendly Models with the Drukhari Trait in any Detachment with the Drukhari Trait in the
    same Army, except Vect himself, that are engaged in a challenge can have the following Gambit
    selected for them:
   The Eye of Vect: If this Gambit is selected, then during the Focus Step the controlling player
   may choose a number between 1 and 3 and apply the penalty to their Focus roll. Then, during the
     Strike Step, the controlling player selects a number of the following characteristics of the Model
    to improve by +1 during that Step, up to the number of the penalty they inflicted to their Focus
      roll: Weapon Skill, Strength, and/or Attacks. If the model does not win the Challenge, then during
    the Glory Step the opposing model automatically gains Challenge Advantage regardless of the
     result.
