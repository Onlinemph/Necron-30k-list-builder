       Special Rules
       Throne of the Dark City
       The Dais of Destruction may only be selected in an Army that includes Asdrubael Vect. If the Days
         of Destruction is selected, Vect must begin the battle Embarked upon it.

       While Vect is Embarked upon the Dais of Destruction, the Dais of Destruction uses Vect's Ballistic
          Skill Characteristic in place of its own.

        Ablation Field
         Attacks with the Armourbane Special Rule that target the Dais of Destruction have a -1 penalty to
          their Strength Characteristic.
