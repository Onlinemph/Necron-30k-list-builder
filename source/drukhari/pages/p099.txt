 Special Rules
 Bones of the Seer
 Once per turn, when Sathonyx’s unit is selected as the Reactive Unit for a Reaction, the controlling
  player can choose to regain 1 point of Reaction Allotment after the Reaction has been resolved.

 The Lord Hellion
 Sathonyx may only join Hellion Gangs and may not join other units.

 One Recon Slot in the same army as Baron Sathonyx may be upgraded to a Prime Slot at no
  additional cost. That Prime Slot must be filled with a Hellion Gang, and the following Prime
 Advantage must be selected for it:

 Twilight Shrouded: Only Sathonyx may join a Unit with this Prime Advantage. All models in the
  Unit with this Prime Advantage gain the Shrouded (5+) Special Rule.
