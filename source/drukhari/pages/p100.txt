       Special Rules
       The Serpent's Venom
            If Sliscus is selected as part of an army, the controlling player can select one Secondary or Apex
       Detachment in that army. The following Prime Advantages can be selected for units in that are
         selected to fill Prime Slots in that Detachment that have either the Infantry or Cavalry Unit Type:

         Sliscus' Dole: If the Unit for which this Prime Advantage is selected does not have the Combat
        Drugs Trait, it gains the [Combat Drugs] Trait. If that Unit already has the [Combat Drug] Trait, then
      when selecting Combat Drugs a second Combat Drug Trait may be selected for that unit.

         Sliscus' Reward: All Weapons with the Splinter Trait used by models in the Unit for which this
       Prime Advantage is selected have their variant of the Poisoned (X) Special Rule replaced with
        Poisoned (2+).
