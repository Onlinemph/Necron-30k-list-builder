                                                                                                 Codex Xenologica – Drukhari (Horus Heresy 3rd edition)



       Special Rules
       Contempt for Death
       At the start of each of the Controlling Player's turns, Urien gains 1 Wound. This cannot bring him
       above his base Wounds value.
       Father of Pain
      When Urien's Fleshcraft (X) Special Rule is activated, an Enemy Model with the Infantry, Cavalry or
       Paragon Type within 6" of Urien may be selected as the Target Model instead of a friendly Model. If
        doing so, and the Intelligence Test is succesful, then the following option must be chosen instead of
        Reknit or Restore:
          •  Ravage: The Target Model's Controlling Player must make a Willpower check for that model
          with a penalty equal to the value of X associated with the variant of Fleshcraft (X) associated
          with the Acting Model. If failed, the Controlling Player of the Acting Model places a Pinned,
          Suppressed, or Stunned Token (their choice) on the Acting Model.
