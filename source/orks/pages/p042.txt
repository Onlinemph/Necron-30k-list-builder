       GARGANTUAN SQUIGGOTH                                      LORD OF WAR
           UNIT COMPOSITION: 1 GARGANTUAN SQUIGGOTH                             550 Points



Just like with other large Battle Fortresses and Stompas, Orks see ownership of a Gargantuan Squiggoth as a sign of a
Warboss’s power and wealth. Gargantuan Squiggoths grow to the size of small Titans and once a howdah has been mounted
upon its back, a Warboss and its Nobz retinue can ride high, shooting down upon the enemy and cheering wildly as the
Squiggoth ploughs through the enemy ranks, goring and trampling on its rampage of destruction.
