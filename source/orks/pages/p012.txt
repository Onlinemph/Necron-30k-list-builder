 WARGEAR
 Grot Oiler
  Grot oilers are the assistants of Ork Meks, and are mostly employed putting out fires or crawling into functioning
  machinery.

 A grot oiler improves the value of X for the Model’s Battlesmith (X) Special Rule.

  Grot oilers are treated as a special kind of Marker that is moved with the Model it belongs to but is ignored for all
 game purposes. A grot oiler may be expended once per Battle during any activation of the owning Model’s Battlesmith
  (X) Special Rule, before making an Intelligence Check for the Acting Model. During this activation of the Battlesmith
  (X) Special Rule, the value of X is improved by +1. The grot oiler Marker is then removed from play. Only a single grot
  oiler may be expended in this way per activation of the Battlesmith (X) Special Rule.
