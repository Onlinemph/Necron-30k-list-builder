The Orks call their ruling caste Nobz. Ork nobility is determined not by some accident of birth or sparkly heirloom but by the
sheer size and belligerence of the Ork claimant. The only Ork bigger and scarier than a Nob is a Warboss, and the Nobz delight
in reminding lesser Orks about their natural supremacy using their scarred fists and iron- shod boots.
