SNAKEBITES

Snakebites are considered backward by the more technologically-minded tribes, for they still follow the old ways. As a result of
their rugged lifestyle, Snakebites are usually weather-beaten and as tough as old boots. They are experts in the field of breeding
stock and their Grots and Squigs are the fiercest in all of Orkdom.
