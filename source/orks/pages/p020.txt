       MEGANOBZ MOB                                                 HEAVY ASSAULT
           UNIT COMPOSITION: 3 MEGANOBZ                                            150 Points
           • May include up to 6 additional Meganobz at +45 Points per Model.


Ork veterans who value the thrill of close combat above all else often become Meganobz. Only the richest of Ork Nobz can
afford to join the ranks of this elite group. They are characterised by the bulky exoskeletons they wear into battle, which they
call mega armour, and by a dogged belief in their own invulnerability.
