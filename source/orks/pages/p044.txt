BAD MOONS

The Bad Moons are the richest of all the Ork clans. This is because their teeth grow faster than anyone else's, meaning that
even the lowliest Bad Moon has a steady supply of wealth. This is not regarded as an unfair advantage, as any Ork big and
nasty enough can simply smash the teeth out of a Bad Moon's head.

All Models with the Bad Moons Trait are subject to the following Special Rules:
