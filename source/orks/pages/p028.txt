Trukks are built for speed and speed alone, and usually have little in the way of armour plating. A direct hit upon a Trukk can
cause it to come apart in a clattering, cartwheeling pile of burning wreckage. Still, because Trukks are so light it is easy to bail
out when they crash, and it is just as likely that a hit will smash apart some wotsit that the driver only hammered into place so
he could keep an eye on it.
