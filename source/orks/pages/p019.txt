        FLASH GITZ MOB                                                                 ELITES
           UNIT COMPOSITION: 3 FLASH GITZ                                      80 Points
           • May include up to 7 additional Flash Gitz at +24 Points per Model.


The richest and most obnoxious of all Orks are indisputably the Flash Gitz. These unsavoury individuals pursue a life of
conquest and pillage, plying the stars in grotesquely ornamented Kill Kroozas and attack craft. The Flash Gitz take every
opportunity they can to fight alongside the rest of the Boyz, if only to flaunt their revoltingly powerful weaponry in front of
their less fortunate brethren.
