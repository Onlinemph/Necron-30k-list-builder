BLOOD AXES

The Blood Axes are held by the other clans to be a bunch of untrustworthy gits. They trade openly with the Imperium, parley
with the foe and will even consider retreating from battle if faced with insurmountable odds. Perhaps once intended to make
the Blood Axes natural leaders, these qualities have instead earned them a reputation as treacherous scumbags.
