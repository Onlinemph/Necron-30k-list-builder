        Repair Barge
       Only Necron Warriors may Embark upon a Ghost Ark. No other model of any kind may Embark
       upon a Ghost Ark.

        In the controlling player’s Movement Phase as the Active Player, when a Model with the Repair
        Barge Special Rule has been selected, but either before or after it has been moved, the controlling
        player of that model may activate this Special Rule. Once activated, the controlling player of the
       model must select one unit of Necron Warriors that is either within 6” of the model or Embarked
       upon it and makes 3 Resurrection (4+) Tests for that unit.
