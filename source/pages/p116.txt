       Special Rules
       Ascend
        At the start of each of the Controlling Player's Turns as the Active Player, starting with that Player's
       Second Turn, that Player rolls a D6. If the result of the D6 is less than the number of the Current
      Game Turn, then Orikan uses the Orikan Empowered Profile until the end of the battle (any lost
       Wounds are carried over to the new Profile).

       By Order of the Triarch
            If Orikan is included in an Army, then Trazyn the Infinite may also be included in that same Army,
        even though they have different versions of the Dynastic Protocols (X) Special Rule.
