       Special Rules
       Time of Bounty
            If Valgûl is included in an Army, the Mark of the Flayer Aeonic Sequela must be selected for that
       Army.

       While Valgûl is on the Battlefield, if an Attack made by Valgûl, a Flayed One model in the same
       Army, or a model with Charnel Displays in the same Army, causes the last Model in an Enemy Unit
         to be removed as a casualty with a Melee Attack, the Controlling Player scores 1 additional Victory
         Points in addition to any Victory Points scored for destroying that Unit.

        Infectious Frenzy
         Valgûl has the Hatred (Challenge Opponent) Special Rule, and all Models in any Unit he joins gains
        the Hatred (Valgûl's Challenge Opponent's Unit) Special Rule.

       While Valgûl is fighting in a Challenge, the Flurry of Blows Gambit must be selected for Models
         in that Challenge. No other Gambit may be selected, and neither player may opt not to select a
        Gambit.
