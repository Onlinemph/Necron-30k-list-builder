       Special Rules
120        Adaptive Tactics
            If Zahndrekh is selected to fill a High Command Slot, one Slot in the same Army may be upgraded
         to become a Prime Slot at no additional cost for every 1000 points of Game Size. Only Troops and
          Elites Slots may be upgraded in this way. If a Unit is selected to fill a Prime Slot upgraded in this
        way, the following Prime Advantage may be selected:

       Superior Maneuvers: The Unit for which this Prime Advantage was selected gains the Infiltrate (9)
         Special Rule and the Ethereal Interception Trait.

        Aeonic Madness
       Zahndrekh must always have a Challenge declared for him if possible and must always be selected
         to accept Challenges if possible, unless Vargard Obyron has joined the same Unit.

        Counter-Strategist
      When resolving a Challenge in which Zahndrekh, or Vargard Obyron who has joined the same Unit
         as Zahndrekh, is taking part, the Opposing Player must always choose the first Gambit in the Face-
        Off of the Challenge Procedure, regardless of which Model is the Challenger and ignoring Challenge
        Advantage.
