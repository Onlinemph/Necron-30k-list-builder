                                                                                                 Codex Xenologica – Necrons (Horus Heresy 3rd edition)

                Necron Unit Types

     C’Tan
     The following rules apply to all models with the
      C’Tan type:

        • A Unit that includes only Models with
        C'Tan Type may ignore the effects of
        any and all Terrain it passes over during
        movement, including passing over Impassable
         Terrain without penalty or restriction, and
          automatically pass Dangerous Terrain Tests.
        However, such Units may not begin or end
          their movement in Impassable Terrain.
        • When targeting a Unit that includes any
        Models with the C'Tan Type, the effects of
         the Poisoned (X) Special Rule only trigger
        on a Wound Test with a result of a '6' before
          modifiers are applied, regardless of the value
          of X for that variant of the Special Rule.
        • A model with the C'tan Type may attack
         with all Weapons they have in each Shooting
         Attack they make, including as part of a
         Reaction. Note that this does not allow
        Weapons without the Assault Trait to be used
          to attack as part of a Volley Attack.
        •  In Step 1 of the Resolution Sub-Phase, when
90       deciding how many Models a Player controls
          in a Combat, a Model with the C'Tan Type
         counts as a number of Models equal to that
         Model's Base Wounds Value.
        • No model may join a C’tan unit.
