 Special Rules
 Mind in the Machine
                                                                                                                          117 When Anrakyr's Unit is selected to make a Shooting Attack, but not when making a Volley Attack or
  firing Snap Shots, his Controlling Player can target one Enemy Model with the Automata, Vehicle
  or Walker Type within 12" and that is visible to Anrakyr. Anrakyr's Controlling Player then takes an
  Intelligence Test for Anrakyr; if the Target Model has the Super-Heavy or Knight Sub-Type, that
  Intelligence Test must be taken by rolling 3D6, rather than 2D6, and if the Target Model has the
  Titan Sub-Type, that Intelligence Test must be taken by rolling 4D6, rather than 2D6. If the test is
  succesful, Anrakyr can make a Shooting Attack using one of the Target Model's Ranged Weapons as
  part of that Shooting Attack as if it was one of his own Ranged Weapons, measuring distances (and,
   if the Target Model is a Vehicle Model, Arcs) from the Target Model rather than Anrakyr.

 Lord of the Pyrrhian Eternals
  If Anrakyr is part of an Army, the Royal Burial Site Aeonic Sequela must be selected for that Army
  (this means Anrakyr always receives the +1 bonus to his Weapon Skill associated with the Royal
  Burial Site Aeonic Sequela).

  If Anrakyr is part of a Crusade Primary Detachment, the Controlling Player selects a Troops Slot in
  the same Detachment. That Troops Slot becomes a Prime Slot. Units of Necron Immortals that are
  selected to fill Prime Slots in the same Army as Anrakyr may have the following Prime Advantage
  selected for them:

 Pyrrhian Eternals: Models in the Unit for which this Prime Advantage was selected gain +1 to their
  Attacks Characteristic. Whilst Anraky has joined the Unit, Models in the Unit also gain the Impact
  (S) Special Rule.


*The +1 to Weapon Skill from the Royal Burial Site Aeonic Sequela is already included in Anrakyr’s
profile.
