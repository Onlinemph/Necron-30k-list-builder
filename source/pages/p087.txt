  Citadel Complex
 The Tomb Citadel is comprised of a large surface with four Fortification emplacements on it. Two
  are for the Tomb Ziggurat and Power Crucible, and two are for the optional Gun emplacements.
 The surface of the Tomb Citadel is treated as being Light Area Terrain. When placing the Tomb
  Ziggurat on the battlefield, the controlling player can place it over other terrain -- when doing so,
 remove any terrain it covers. The Citadel is so large that it may not fit in the controlling player’s
 Deployment Zone; if that is the case, deploy the Tomb Citadel so that as much of it as possible is
  within the controlling player’s Deployment Zone. The Tomb Citadel may have to be deployed first,
  so other units may be deployed on top of it.

  If any Fortification that is part of the Tomb Citadel is destroyed, that model remains on the
  battlefield as Impassible Terrain. The Gun Emplacement locations are also Impassible Terrain if no
 Gun Emplacement was purchased for it.
