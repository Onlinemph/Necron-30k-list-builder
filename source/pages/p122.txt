     Wargear
        Splinter of Madness
            If a Unit that includes any Models with this Special Rule would gain the Routed Status, then that
        Unit suffers 3 Wounds against which only Invulnerable Saves may be taken and Damage Mitigation
         Rolls may not be taken, and it does not gain the Routed Status.

       Charnel Phylactery
        Kutlakh has the Ever-Living (2+) Special Rule. At the end of any Challenge Sub-Phase or Initiative
        Step in which Kutlakh's Current Wounds Characteristic is lower than his Starting Wounds
         Characteristic, if he currently has the Ever-Living (X) Special Rule, he loses the Ever-Living (X)
         Special Rule until the end of the Battle (and cannot regain that Special Rule for any reason, even if
       he gains Wounds later in the battle) and his Attacks Characteristic is increased by +2 until the end of
        the Battle.

       The Godslayer's Ire
       While a Model with this Special Rule is engaged in a Challenge, its Controlling Player can select the
         following Gambit for that Model:

      The Godslayer's Ire: If this Gambit is selected, then the Current Willpower Characteristic of
        each Model involved in the Challenge is used in place of that Model's Combat Initiative when
        determining the bonus to the Focus Roll.
