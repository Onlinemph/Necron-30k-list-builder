        Psionic Shockwave
        During Step 10 of the Initiative Step in which a Model makes Attacks using this Weapon, take note
         of the number of Unsaved Wounds inflicted by Attacks made with this Weapon. For each Unit that
         suffered Unsaved Wounds, that Unit must immediately take a Willpower Check with a penalty
        equal to the number of Unsaved Wounds lost. If failed, that Unit immediately gains the Suppressed
         Tactical Status. This Special Rule has no effect when fighting in a Challenge.
