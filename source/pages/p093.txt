               Aeonic Sequelae

Sequelae Benefits
An Army which makes use of the Necron Army List may have a number of Aeonic Sequelae from
the following list selected for it which apply to every Detachment in that Army which includes Units
selected from the Necron Army List, each providing additional access to additional Detachments,
Special Rules to Models, and options for Army Selection:

• Awoken Vaults
•  Canoptek Forge
•  Cult of Annihilation
•  Dark Harvest
•  Forsaken Voidfarers
•  Horrors of Old
•  Hypercrypt Legion
• Mark of the Flayer
•  Royal Burial Site
•  Servants of the Ankh
•  Slumbering Tombs
• The Symbiosis

If an Army contains at least one Unit selected from the Necrons Army List then a single Aeonic Sequela
may be selected for that Army, with one additional Aeonic Sequela also available for selection if the
Army includes one or more Models with the Nemesor or Phaeron Trait.
