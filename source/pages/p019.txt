        Necrontyr Crypto-Arkana Rules

All Crypteks in the Necron Army List will have a Crypto-Arkana represented by a Trait on its Unit
Entry. For Models which are not specific to a Crypto-Arkana, this will be represented by '[Crypto-
Arkana]'. When such a Unit is added to an Army, one of the following Crypto-Arkanas must be selected
to replace this with.

•  Chronomancy
•  Ethermancy
• Geomancy
•  Plasmancy
•  Psychomany
•  Technomancy

Models which are specific to a certain Crypto-Arkana will already have the appropriate Trait.

Each Crypto-Arkana Trait grants the Model that has it one additional Harbinger Special Rule, as well
as one Crypto-Arkana Weapons List and one Techno-Arkana List corresponding to that Crypto-
Arkana.
