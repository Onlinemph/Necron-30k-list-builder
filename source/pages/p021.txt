                  Crypto-Arkana – Ethermancy

Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
Ethermancy Trait.


 Harbinger of the Storm
 A model with the Ethermancy Trait gains the Shrouded (4+) Special Rule.

 When a friendly Unit is selected as a Target of a Shooting Attack (including a Shooting Attack made
  as part of a Reaction), during Step 2 of the Shooting Attack Process, the Controlling Player may
  select one friendly model in that Unit or within 6" of that Unit that has the Ethermancy Trait. The
  Controlling Player makes an Intelligence Check for the Selected Model. If succesful, the Unit that
 was targeted for that Shooting Attack gains the Shrouded (4+) Special Rule until Step 11 of that
  Shooting Attack has been resolved. If the Check is failed then the unit that includes the model
  that just failed the Test gains the Stunned Status. Each model may only be selected to make an
  Intelligence Check for this Special Rule once per turn.
