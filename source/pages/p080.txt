      The Grand Illusion
        At the end of the Deploy Armies Step of any Mission Sequence, if an army includes a Model with
          this Special Rule, that player can select up to one of their own Units and one opposing Units and
        re-deploy them, following any Deployment Restrictions that Unit has to follow. This can be used
        to re-deploy a Unit in Reserves on the Battlefield, or place a Unit on the Battlefield in Reserves.
        Units inside a transport may not be re-deployed, nor may this rule be used to Embark Units inside
        a Transport, but the Transport itself may be re-deployed in which case any units Embarked inside
         that Transport remain inside the Transport. Units that include any models with the Paragon Type
       and Transports that have any Paragon models Embarked upon them may not be re-deployed using
          this Special Rule.
