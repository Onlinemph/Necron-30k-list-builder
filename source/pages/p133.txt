               FAQ

Veil of Darkness
Q. Can a unit deployed using the Veil of Darkness
declare a charge in the same turn?
A. Yes, unless it is that unit’s first turn as
described in the Infiltrate special rule.

Horrors of the Old War
The following units are available to an Army
with the Horrors of Old Aeonic Sequela.
• High Command: Necron Overlord.
• HQ: Necron Lord, Destroyer Lord in Lokhust
   Frame.
•  Elites: Pariahs.
•  Troops: Necron Warriors, Immortals.
•  Support: Canoptek Spyders, Canoptek Tomb
   Sentinel, Lokhust Destroyers, Lokhust Heavy
   Destroyers.
• Heavy Assault: Flayed Ones, Canoptek
  Tomb Stalker.
•  Recon: Canoptek Scarabs, Canoptek
   Wraiths.
•  Fast Attack: Ophydian Destroyers.
•  Lord of War: Monolith, Gauss Pylon.
