 Gravity Pulse
 Once per Battle only, the Controlling Player of a Model with this Special Rule can activate its
  Gravity Pulse. The Gravity Pulse can be Activated when this Model is selected to make Shooting
  Attacks (excepting Volley Attacks), including as part of a Reaction, and is resolved before this
  Model's Shooting Attacks. The Targets of the Gravity Pulse are all Enemy Units that that are within
  24" of this Model. The Controlling Player rolls a D6 for each Target of the Gravity Pulse, with a
  Target Number of '4+' for Targets that include any Models with the Antigrav or Flyer Sub-Type and
  '6' for Targets that do not. Each Unit for which the Target Number is rolled gains the Pinned Tactical
  Status.

  Units that include any Models with the Gargantuan, Knight, Super-Heavy or Titan Sub-Type cannot
 be Targets of the Gravity Pulse and are unaffected.
