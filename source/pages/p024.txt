                      Crypto-Arkana – Psychomancy

      Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
      Psychomancy Trait.


       Harbinger of Despair
     A model with the Psychomancy Trait gains the Fear (1) Special Rule.

      When a Unit without the Vehicle Type controlled by the Opposing Player gains the Pinned,
        Stunned, or Suppressed Status, the Controlling Player of a Model with the Psychomancy Trait
        within 12" of that Unit may select that model. The Controlling Player makes an Intelligence Check
         for the selected model. If the Check is succesful, the Controlling Player of the Model with the
       Psychomancy Trait may choose to have the Unit controlled by the Opposing Player gain one
         additional Status from among Pinned, Stunned, and Suppressed. If the Check is failed then the unit
         that includes the model that just failed the Test gains the Stunned Status. Each model may only be
        selected to make an Intelligence Check for this Special Rule once per turn.
