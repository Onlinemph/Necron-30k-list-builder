      Wargear
       Cloak of the Burning Mind
124            If an Enemy Unit fails a Morale Test while engaged with Gorz's Unit, that Unit suffers D3 automatic
       Wounds against which only Invulnerable Saves may be made (Damage Mitigation rolls may not be
       made against those Wounds). Wounds inflicted this way are allocated by the Opposing Player.



       Special Rules
       Canoptek Minions
        Whilst Gorz has joined a Unit, all Models with the Canoptek Trait in that Unit have their Ballistic
          Skill Characteristic modified by +1. If Gorz allows another Unit to make a Reaction using the
        Harbinger of the Swarm Special Rule, Models with the Canoptek Trait in that Unit have their
          Ballistic Skill Characteristic modified by +1 until that Reaction is fully resolved.

       Decoherent Mind
       Any Unit joined by Gorz may not Rush and must make a Shooting Attack in the Shooting Phase if
         there is any available Target within the Maximum Range Gorz's Meteor Staff. When Gorz makes a
        Shooting Attack with his Meteor Staff, if a '1' is rolled when making the To Hit Test, the Opposing
         Player may move the Blast Template anywhere on the Battlefield before the Template is Scattered.
