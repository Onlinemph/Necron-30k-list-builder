                                                                                                  Codex Xenologica – Necrons (Horus Heresy 3rd edition)


       Special Rules
        Flense Alive
       While a Model with this Special Rule is engaged in a Challenge, its Controlling Player can select the
         following Gambit for that Model:

       Flense Alive: If this Gambit is selected, any Hits caused by the Model for which it was selected
        during the Strike Step have their Damage Characteristic set to '1' and cannot have their Damage
         Characteristic modified by any Special Rule. During the Glory Step, if the Enemy Model has
         suffered any Unsaved Wounds and has not been removed as a casualty, its Toughness Characteristic
           is modified by -1 (to a minimum of 1) until the end of the battle and the Model for which this
        Challenge was selected automatically gains Challenge Advantage.

        Systematic Extermination
        At the start of battle, Xun'Bakyr's controlling player may select one Enemy Unit on the Battlefield.
       That Unit is the 'Extermination Target' for the purpose of this Special Rule. Each time a Model with
        the Reanimant Trait makes a Melee or Ranged Attack targeting the Extermination Target, add 1 to
        the Strength or Ranged Strength of the Attack, even if Xun'Bakyr has been removed as a casualty.

            If the Extermination Target is entirely removed as a casualty, and Xun'Bakyr has not been
       removed as a casualty, the Controlling Player may immediately select a new Enemy Unit to be the
        Extermination Target.

      The Phaerakh's Horde
            If Xun'Bakyr is part of an Army, the Dark Harvest Aeonic Sequela must be selected for that Army.

       While Xun'Bakyr remains on the Battlefield, all Units with the Reanimant Trait and Troops
          Battlefield Role in the same Army gain the Implacable Advance and Firestorm Special Rule.
