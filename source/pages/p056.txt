      Translocation Portal
     A model equipped with a Translocation Portal can be assigned the following Combat Assignment:

        Invasion Mission
          • Once assigned an Invasion Mission in the Reserves Sub-Phase, a model must be immediately
          placed onto the Battlefield by the Controlling player. The Model may be placed anywhere along
          the Battlefield Edge that is within the Controlling Player's Deployment Zone. This deployment
          can trigger the Intercept Advanced Reaction, but any attacks made at the Model are made as
         Snap Shots.
          •  In the Movement Phase, a model with an Invasion Mission can be selected and must then move
         up to a number of inches equal to its Movement Characteristic straight forwards without turning.
          At this point a Combat Air Patrol can be declared by the Reactive Player. Once any Combat Air
           Patrol Reactions have been resolved, the Controlling Player may select a Unit entirely composed
           of models with the Infantry Type in reserves that has no more than 15 models (counting models
          with the Bulky (X) special rule as a number of models equal to the value of X attached to that
           special rule) that is in reserves or on the battlefield wholly within 12" of the Model with the
           Invasion Mission and apply the following depending on that unit's location:
              -- In Reserves: Place the unit anywhere on the battlefield wholly within 12" of the base of the
           Model with the Invasion Mission. A unit placed on the battlefield this way cannot triger
             Reactions and cannot move further in the current Movement Phase but is counted as having
          moved for the purpose of any rules and special rules.
              -- On the Battlefield: Remove the unit from the Battlefield and place it in Reserves.
          •  In the Shooting Phase, a model that has been assigned an Invasion Mission may make Shooting
           Attacks, but must make all attacks as Snap Shots. At the end of the Controlling Player's Shooting
          Phase, the Model assigned an Invasion Mission is removed from play and returned to Aerial
           Reserves.
