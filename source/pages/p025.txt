                  Crypto-Arkana – Technomancy

Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
Technomancy Trait.


 Harbinger of the Swarm
 The value in brackets of the Battlesmith (X) Special Rule of a model with the Harbinger of the
 Swarm Special Rule is modified by +1. If the model does not already have the Battlesmith (X) Special
  Rule, it gains the Battlesmith (1) Special Rule instead. Models with the Harbinger of the Swarm
  Special Rule that can join Units may join Units that include Models with the Automata Type.

 When a Friendly Unit with that includes any models with the Canoptek Trait would be eligible to
 make a Reaction, but cannot because it includes models with the Automata Type, the Controlling
  Player may Select one model in that Unit or within 6" of that Unit that has the Technomancy
  Trait. The controlling player makes an Intelligence Check for the selected model. If the Check is
  succesful, the Controlling Player of the Unit that would be eligible to React may make a Reaction
  with that Unit, and does not have to pay the Cost of that Reaction to do so. If the Check is failed
  then the unit that includes the model that just failed the Test gains the Stunned Status. Each model
 may only be selected to make an Intelligence Check for this Special Rule once per turn.
