                       Crypto-Arkana – Geomancy

      Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
     Geomancy Trait.


       Harbinger of Transmogrification
      A model with the Geomancy Trait gains the Phasing Trait.

      When a friendly Unit is selected during the Controlling Player's Movement Phase as the Active
         Player, but before or after that Unit has been Moved, the Controlling Player may Select one model
         in that Unit or within 6" of that Unit that has the Geomancy Trait. The Controlling Player makes
       an Intelligence Check for the selected model. If the Check is succesful that Unit gains +3 to its
      Movement Characteristic, the Phase Motion special rule and the Phasing Trait until the start of the
        Active Player's next Turn. If the Check is failed then the unit that includes the model that just failed
        the Test gains the Stunned Status. Each model may only be selected to make an Intelligence Check
         for this Special Rule once per turn.
