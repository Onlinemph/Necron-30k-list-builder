 Canoptek Hegemon
  Units from the same Army within 12" of a Model with this Special Rule that include Automata
 Models can make Reactions, even though they include Automata models, so long as all Automata
  models in the Unit have the Canoptek Trait.


  Tides of Hate
 Once per battle only, instead of rolling to randomly determine a Power of the C'Tan, this model's
  Controlling Player may choose to have this model use the following unique Power of the C'Tan:

  Tides of Hate: When using the Tides of Hate Power of the C'Tan, this model’s controlling player
  selects an enemy unit within 12” of this Model. Remove all Condition Tokens from the selected
  Unit, if any. This Model’s controlling player may make a Shooting Attacks this Shooting Phase using
  that unit, as if that Unit was part of that Player's Army. The Unit is counted as being disengaged for
  the purpose of making that Shooting Attack.
