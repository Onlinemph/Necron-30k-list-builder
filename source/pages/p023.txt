                   Crypto-Arkana – Plasmancy

Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
Geomancy Trait.


 Harbinger of Destruction
 A model with the Plasmancy Trait gains the Firing Protocols (2) and Firestorm Special Rules.

  At the start of Step 4 of any Shooting Attack made by a friendly Unit, the Controlling Player may
  Select one model in that Unit or within 6" of that Unit that has the Plasmancy Trait. The Controlling
  Player makes an Intelligence Check for the selected model. If the Check is succesful then until the
 end of the Shooting Attack being resolved all Ranged Weapons used by models in the attacking Unit
  gain +1 to their Ranged Strength Characteristic and the Breaching (6+) Special Rule. If the Check is
  failed then the unit that includes the model that just failed the Test gains the Stunned Status. Each
 model may only be selected to make an Intelligence Check for this Special Rule once per turn.
