 Wargear
 Ghostwalk Mantle
 During the Aftermath Step of the Resolution Sub-Phase, the following Aftermath Option can be
  selected for a Unit that includes any Model with the Ghostwalk Mantle and does not includes any          121
 Models with the Routed Status:

 Ghostwalk: All models in the Unit are removed from the Battlefield, then the Unit is re-deployed
 anywhere on the Battlefield anywhere as if they were deployed using the Infiltrate (9) Special Rule.
  If the Unit had lost the Combat, the Opposing Player may choose to have any Units under their
  control Consolidate or Hold.


 Special Rules
  Cleaving Counterblow
 While a Model with this Special Rule is engaged in a Challenge, its Controlling Player can select the
  following Gambit for that Model:

 Cleaving Counterblow: If this Gambit is selected, then the Model controlled by the Player
 who selected this Gambit has a -2 penalty to its Focus Roll and receives a +2 bonus to its Attacks
  Characteristic in the Strike Step.

  Obyron's Duty
 Obyron gains the Ever-Living (5+) Special Rule so long as he has joined the same Unit as Nemesor
  Zahndrekh. While Obyron has joined the same Unit as Nemesor Zahndrekh, he must always have a
  Challenge declared for him if possible and must always be selected to accept Challenges if possible.
