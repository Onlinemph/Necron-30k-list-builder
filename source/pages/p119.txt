 Psy-Shock
  If a Shooting Attack made for a Weapon or Model with this Special Rule inflicts one or more
 wounds on a Target Unit which contains any Models with the Psyker Trait or Malefic Sub-Type,
  regardless of whether any Damage is inflicted, that Target Unit suffers the Warp Rupture resulton
  the Perils of the Warp Table at the end of Step 9 of that Shooting Attack.


 Special Rules
 Atomic Manipulation
  At the end of Step 8 of the Initiative Step in which Szeras Attacks or Step 4 of the Challenge
  Sub-Phase in which Szeras makes Strikes, his Controlling Player may select one Unit of Necron
  Warriors or Necron Immortals from their Army and within 12" of Szeras and make a number of
  Resurrection (4+) rolls for that Unit equal to the total amount of Unsaved Damage inflicted by Szeras
  during the current Step.

 Mechanical Augmentation
  All Troops Slots in the same Detachment as Szeras become Prime Slots. The following Prime
  Advantage must be selected for Units that are selected to fill those Prime Slots:

 Mechanical Augmentation: At the Start of Battle, the Controlling Player of a Unit with this Prime
  Advantage rolls a D6 and applies the following Benefit to this Unit for the duration of the battle,
  depending on the result of the Dice Roll:
  •  1-2: Hardened Carapace: +1 Toughness.
  •  3-4: Improved Optics: +1 Ballistic Skill.
  •  5-6: Enhanced Servomotors: +1 Strength.
