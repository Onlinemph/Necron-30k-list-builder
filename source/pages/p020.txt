                      Crypto-Arkana – Chronomancy

      Below are listed the Special Rule, Wargear Items and Weapons accessible to models with the
     Chronomancy Trait.


       Harbinger of Eternity
     A model with the Chronomancy Trait gains the Phasing Trait.

      When a friendly Unit is selected as the Reacting Unit for a Reaction, the controlling player may
        Select one model in that Unit or within 6" of that Unit that has the Chronomancy Trait. The
        Controlling Player makes an Intelligence Check for the selected model. If the Check is succesful
        the Controlling Player gains 1 point of Reaction Allotment immediately before the Cost for that
        Reaction is paid. If the Check is failed then the unit that includes the model that just failed the Test
         gains the Stunned Status. Each model may only be selected to make an Intelligence Check for this
        Special Rule once per turn.
