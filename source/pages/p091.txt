        Necron Auxiliary Detachments

An army can include a number of Auxiliary and Apex Detachments, depending on the number and type
of Command Choices selected.

In addition to the Auxiliary Detachments in the Age of Darkness Rulebook [3rd edition], armies
which make use of the Necron Army List have access to a number of additional Auxiliary and Apex
Detachments. These follow all the standard rules for Auxilliary Detachments, but may have certain
restrictions upon which unit is used to fill the Command or High Command Force Organization Slot
which allows their selection.


        Flexible Slots: a blank Battlefield Role icon in a Detachment indicates a Flexible Slot. A
         Flexible Slot may be filled using Units of more than one Battlefield Role. Each Detachment
         that has Flexible Slots will describe which Units can be included as part of the Flexible Slot.
